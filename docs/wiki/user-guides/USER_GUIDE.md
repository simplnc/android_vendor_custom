# 👥 User Guide - Simple Overview

## 🌟 **What is This?**

This is a **Custom LineageOS Vendor Configuration** that replaces the default apps on your Android phone with **29 privacy-focused, open-source alternatives**. Think of it as upgrading your phone with better, more secure apps.

## 🎯 **What You Get**

### **🔒 Privacy & Security**
- **No tracking** - Apps don't collect your personal data
- **Local processing** - Everything happens on your device
- **Open source** - You can see exactly what the apps do
- **No ads** - Clean, distraction-free experience

### **📱 Better Apps**
- **Modern design** - Beautiful, up-to-date interfaces
- **More features** - Enhanced functionality over default apps
- **Better performance** - Faster, more efficient apps
- **Customization** - Extensive personalization options

## ❓ **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: Is this safe?**
**A:** Yes! All apps are open-source and privacy-focused. You can verify what they do.

### **Q: Will this work on my phone?**
**A:** This works with LineageOS 18.1+ on Android 12+ devices.

### **Q: Can I uninstall these apps?**
**A:** Some apps are system apps, but most can be disabled or replaced.

### **Q: What if I don't like an app?**
**A:** You can install alternatives from F-Droid or Google Play Store.

### **Q: Will this slow down my phone?**
**A:** No, these apps are optimized for performance and often faster than defaults.
