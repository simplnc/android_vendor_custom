# British Cancer Research - Charity Partnership

## 🎗️ Our Commitment

We are proud to support [British Cancer Research](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/) in their mission to beat cancer sooner. As part of our commitment to giving back to the community, we donate a portion of our development profits to support cancer research and treatment.

## 💰 Donation Structure

### Current Commitment
- **2% of Development Profits**: Donated quarterly to British Cancer Research
- **Transparent Reporting**: All donations are publicly documented
- **Scalable Impact**: Donations increase with project success

### Future Scaling
As our project grows and generates more revenue, we will scale our donations accordingly:

| Revenue Range | Donation Percentage | Additional Initiatives |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| £0 - £1,000 | 2% | Basic support |
| £1,000 - £10,000 | 3% | Monthly updates |
| £10,000 - £50,000 | 5% | Research partnerships |
| £50,000+ | 7% | Major research funding |

## 🏥 Why British Cancer Research?

### Mission Alignment

### Impact Areas
- **Early Detection**: Developing better screening methods

- **Patient Care**: Supporting cancer patients
