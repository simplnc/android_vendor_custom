# Donation Policy - Developer Compensation & App Promotion

## 🎯 Overview

This document outlines our policy for compensating open-source app developers whose applications are included in our LineageOS vendor package. We believe in fair compensation for developers and transparent promotion of their work.

## 💰 Developer Compensation Structure

### Base Compensation
- **Per-App Fee**: £50-200 per application included
- **Usage-Based**: Additional compensation based on ROM distribution
- **Update Support**: Ongoing payments for app updates

### Compensation Tiers

| App Category | Base Fee | Usage Bonus | Update Fee |
|-------------|----------|-------------|------------|
| Core System Apps | £200 | £0.10/user | £50/update |
| Productivity Apps | £150 | £0.08/user | £40/update |
| Utility Apps | £100 | £0.05/user | £30/update |
| Experimental Apps | £50 | £0.03/user | £20/update |

### Payment Schedule
- **Initial Payment**: 50% upon inclusion
- **Release Payment**: 50% upon ROM release
- **Usage Payments**: Quarterly based on user count
- **Update Payments**: Per update cycle
