# Regulatory Compliance Guide - Enterprise Compliance Framework

## 🎯 Overview

This comprehensive guide provides detailed regulatory compliance analysis for the Android Vendor Custom project, covering all aspects from data protection to industry standards.

## 🏛️ Regulatory Compliance Strategy

### Compliance Levels
1. **Data Protection Compliance** - GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, etc.
2. **Security Compliance** - ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST, etc.
3. **Privacy Compliance** - Privacy laws and regulations
4. **Industry Compliance** - Healthcare, Finance, Government, etc.
5. **Regional Compliance** - Country-specific regulations
6. **Enterprise Compliance** - Corporate governance and standards
7. **Technical Compliance** - Technical standards and requirements
8. **Operational Compliance** - Operational procedures and controls
